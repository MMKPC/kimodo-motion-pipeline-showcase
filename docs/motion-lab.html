What this proves

A director-facing control surface, not a fake model demo.

The live skeleton is a deterministic procedural visualization built for this public showcase. It demonstrates interaction, review controls, motion vocabulary, validation gates, and the shape of a production handoff. Model inference, private weights, local paths, and DCC automation remain protected.
